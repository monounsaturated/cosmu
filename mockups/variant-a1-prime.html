Run these in a Claude Code session connected to the Cosmu workspace.

/run-gate
Evaluate the current strategy cohort through the deterministic Gate. Produces pass/fail verdicts with DSR, PBO, drawdown, and trade count.
/dump-idea <thesis>
Turn a natural-language trading idea into a typed StrategySpec and drop it in the inbox for the next Gate cohort.
/create-strategy
Author a new strategy spec from scratch, flowing it through the Gate and into a paper track if it passes.
/manage-data
Fetch, backfill, and verify market bars + alt-data sources. Reports coverage, staleness, and missing gaps.
/evolve-strategy <id>
Isolate a gate-passed strategy's winning signal and graft it onto other assets or recombine with survivors.
/scan-signals
Score all registered alt-data signals on live data and surface the top predictive candidates for the next cohort.
/align-check
Strategic alignment review — measures recent work against the north star (autonomous profit, net of fees) and flags drift.
/deploy-check
Pre-push gate — runs pnpm verify and confirms the tree is green before deploying.
