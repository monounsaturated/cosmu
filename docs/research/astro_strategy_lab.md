## Honest note

With N trials, the expected best raw Sharpe under PURE NOISE is ~`sqrt(2·ln N)/sqrt(T)` annualized — a large number by construction. The Deflated Sharpe charges for exactly that selection; a config only matters if DSR>0.95 AND it then survives a forward (out-of-sample) test. Raw Sharpe alone is the false-positive machine.
